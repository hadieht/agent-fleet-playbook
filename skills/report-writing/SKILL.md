# Report writing

## Procedure
1. **Reader and form:** who reads it, what they'll do with it, and the format and length that fits (exec brief ≤ 1 page, report, email, article).
2. **Read the raw sources:** `00-request.md`, every stage `output.md`, `verify.md`, `critique.md`.
3. **Throughline:** one sentence that the whole document supports.
4. **Structure:** answer first (BLUF), then supporting points, then risks and open items, then next steps.
5. **Write** plainly, with short sentences, concrete numbers with units, and one idea per paragraph.
6. **Fidelity pass:** every number, name, date and claim traces to a stage output. Mark anything else, or remove it.
7. **Uncertainty pass:** keep the confidence levels and the open items. Don't smooth them away.

## Output format
```
## Form
## Document
## Fidelity note    # condensed / dropped / unverifiable
## Suggested next use
```

## Rules
- No new facts.
- Readability beats exhaustiveness.
- Sending or publishing goes through `approve`.
